Fiction2Reality

Fiction2Reality (F2R) is a long-term, multi-stage venture I founded to move fictional worlds into our own, starting with digital media and, eventually, physical places people can experience.

Visit Fiction2Reality.com‎ ↗

Turning Fictional Places into Explorable Spaces

Fiction2Reality began with a question: What would it take to create a complete currently-fictional setting as a place someone could explore; not merely a façade, a ride, a film set, or collection of disconnected rooms, but as the fully realized place?

That question first centered on Hogwarts, and later expanded to the Backrooms and liminal spaces. Fiction2Reality now brings these interests together through digital storytelling, with 3D environments, 2D animation, short films, and long-term venture planning for real-life, visitable buildings.

Any future work involving protected fictional properties would require appropriate licensing and/or partnerships. For now, the project is building its creative foundation through digital work and collaboration.

From Digital Concepts to Physical Places

Fiction2Reality is designed to grow in stages, rather than leap directly from an idea to a building.

  1. 01

    Digital Creations

    Develop proof-of-concept media through 3D renders, animation, short films, writing, and other original media.

  2. 02

    Business Development

    Research feasibility, costs, audiences, partnerships, licensing, operations, and sustainable ways to grow.

  3. 03

    Physical Experiences

    Work toward real-world, explorable environments, beginning with a real-life explorable Backrooms building.

Building the Proof-of-Concept

Fiction2Reality is currently creating and organizing the digital work that can communicate the idea and grow a community around it. That includes Backrooms and other “fictions” videos, early 3D environment experiments, stories and scripts, video games, website and brand development, and collaboration with others.
