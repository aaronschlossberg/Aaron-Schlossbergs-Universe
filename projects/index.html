My Big Projects & Entrepreneurial Ventures

Welcome! I'm excited to share the innovative projects that I'm currently working on, alongside friends like Matan Rieger, Luna, Alexander Lee, and others. I'm developing a range of entrepreneurial ventures and large projects that combine my passion for entrepreneurship, technology, 3D modeling, and various other topics. Below is an overview of my projects.

Current Projects:

Overview:
• Info Sprawlings: The Info Sprawlings is a growing system of connected, reusable knowledge pages about software, concepts, people, places, projects, and other subjects, built to make substantial explanations easier to explore, relate, and reuse.
• Mayvene: A Q&A (question-and-answer) site being designed to improve knowledge-sharing in a more structured and community-driven way.
• Fiction2Reality: Fiction2Reality is a long-term creative venture I founded to turn fictional worlds into immersive, explorable experiences; beginning with 3D environments, animation, short films, and worldbuilding, and ultimately working toward physical experiences.

In-depth:
The Info Sprawlings is a growing web of substantial knowledge pages, with one subject at the center of each page and explicit connections among related topics, terms, writings, and projects. It begins here as part of Aaron Schlossberg’s Universe, while being structured so that its knowledge can eventually grow into a larger independent resource and potentially connect with Mayvene. Explore the Info Sprawlings


Mayvene: We are in the early stages of developing an innovative question-and-answer (Q&A) platform. Our mission is, “Spreading knowledge, life experiences, and gaining a better understanding of the universe around us”. Our goal is to create a space where users can ask and answer questions. And especially with the rise of AI chatbots nowadays, it's important to have a site like this run by real people, because AI cannot foster a community of shared knowledge, expertise, and different perspectives like real people can, and AI is often prone to hallucination. And we also will have helpful features, utilize techniques to mitigate trolls and spam, and deploy non-paywall nor heavily ad-reliant site revenue techniques to help keep the site alive.




Fiction2Reality (F2R)

Fiction2Reality explores how fictional worlds can become places people can actually enter and experience. The project is currently in its digital proof-of-concept stage, creating 3D environments, animation, short films, original worldbuilding around the Backrooms, liminal spaces, and other fictional settings, and early business development. Its longer-term roadmap includes partnerships, licensing, and eventually physical experiences.
