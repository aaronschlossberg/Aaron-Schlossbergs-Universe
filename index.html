Goals

Beyond business and technology, he has a profound desire to create meaningful change. He wants to contribute to solving global issues such as hunger and homelessness, while also working toward advancements like radical longevity. He doesn't concern himself with ideological debates or rigid political labels — he simply wants to do what's right. These lyrics of Fly Like an Eagle by Steve Miller Band resonate deeply with him:

“I want to feed the babies
Who can't get enough to eat
I want to shoe the children
New shoes on their feet
I want to house the people
Living in the street
Oh, there, there's a solution...”

...He wants to help those who don't have enough to eat, provide shoes for those who have none, provide shelter to those without a home, and improve peoples lives in other tangible ways. Instead of getting caught up in debates over "who is right" vs "who is wrong"... his focus is on taking action and creating solutions; just DOING what's right!

Ultimately, he lives by these words of Alan Kay: “The best way to predict the future is to invent it.” Aaron's overarching goal is simple yet ambitious: “I want to leave the world at least 1% better than I joined it.” And through his entrepreneurial ventures, creative projects, and constant drive to innovate, he is determined and motivated to make that happen.
